# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want **SC Payslip** to be a welcoming, professional, and safe place to collaborate—whether you are here to use the tool, report a bug, suggest an improvement, or contribute code.

We are committed to building a community where people can participate **without harassment**, **without hostility**, and **without personal attacks**.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, kind, and constructive
- Giving clear feedback and focusing on the problem, not the person
- Assuming good intent and asking clarifying questions
- Accepting differences of opinion and discussing them professionally
- Helping new contributors get started (even with small improvements)

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in this project:

- Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, or derogatory comments
- Personal attacks, insults, mocking, or aggressive behavior
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention
- Threats, intimidation, or encouraging others to harass someone
- Doxxing or sharing private information without permission
- Repeated disruption (trolling, spamming, derailing threads)
- Any attempt to leak secrets, tokens, credentials, or exploit the project

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions related to this repository, including:

- Issues
- Pull requests
- Code reviews
- Discussions
- Comments and community communication connected to the project

---

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the maintainers.

**How to report:**
- Use GitHub’s reporting tools where available
- If the issue is sensitive, contact the maintainer privately (e.g., via GitHub profile contact options)

**Please include:**
- What happened (summary)
- Links/screenshots (if available)
- Who was involved
- When it happened
- Any additional context that helps investigate

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate to keep the community safe and productive, including:

- Removing or editing content
- Requesting changes to behavior
- Locking or closing discussions
- Temporarily limiting interactions
- Restricting access or banning users from the repository

Maintainers will prioritize safety, fairness, and the long-term health of the community.

---

## Respectful Collaboration Rules (Simple Version)

- Be helpful, not hostile  
- Debate ideas, not people  
- Keep it professional  
- No harassment, no spam  
- Security issues get reported privately (see `SECURITY.md`)
